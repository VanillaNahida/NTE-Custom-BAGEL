# Features

- **Cross-server compatible**: Works on both CN and Global servers, no version distinction needed.
- **Multi-resolution support**: Supports photo replacement for 720P, 1080P, and 1440P window resolutions.
- **Automatic image processing**: Automatically scales selected images to fit the game's resolution — no manual editing required.

# How to Use

Tutorial video: [https://www.xcnahida.cn/archives/MFb9rQFg](https://www.xcnahida.cn/archives/MFb9rQFg)

## Instructions

1. Do NOT launch the game launcher first. Start `NTE-Custom-BAGEL.exe` instead. You can download it from the [Release](https://github.com/VanillaNahida/NTE-Custom-BAGEL/releases) page.

2. Select the image you want to upload, then click the Start button. A black console window will appear — do NOT close it.

3. After entering the launcher, go to the launcher settings and enable **DX11** to launch the game, otherwise the injection will definitely fail.

4. Launch the game through the launcher. When `HTGame.exe running. You can keep this window open or close it.` appears in the `launcher.exe` window, the injection was successful.

5. In-game, enter Photo Mode and press **F9** to start capture/replacement. Take your photo, then press **F9** again to stop capture/replacement.

6. If you are unable to log in consistently, please restart the game.

## Important Notes

- **DO NOT press F9 before entering Photo Mode**, or you will face consequences at your own risk.
- To change images, simply click the Select Image button in the main interface — the injector will automatically replace it.
- After entering Photo Mode, press **F9** to start capture/replacement, take your photo, then press **F9** again to stop.

> [!WARNING]
> If you do not press **F9** to cancel the injection after taking your photo, all images in your interface will be replaced!

- Only supports **720P**, **1080P**, and **1440P** window resolution photos. Other resolutions will cause the replacement to fail, and larger images cannot be uploaded.
- If you are unable to log in consistently, please restart the game.
